Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

This policy explains what information Hospital Roster Builder handles and how it is used to provide roster planning.

Information used by the workspace

When you sign in, the service processes your account email and authentication details to verify access. Staff names, optional employee IDs, roster titles, selected month and year, and duty assignments may be stored with the workspace so authorized users can manage and reopen schedules.

Information saved in your browser

Some display and editing preferences, custom roster titles or duty codes, and extra blank roster rows may be stored in this browser. Clearing browser site data can remove those local items. A roster saved through the Save Roster action is stored with the workspace account.

How information is used

Information is used to authenticate users, show staff and saved rosters, and create roster views and exports requested by the user. The app does not need roster information for advertising.

Access and sharing

People who can access the same workspace account may be able to view its staff directory and saved rosters. PDF, Excel, print, and share actions are initiated by the user; check a file or recipient before distributing it.

Retention and requests

Cloud information remains available while it is retained by the workspace service or account. Contact the developer to request help with account access or data questions. Some records may need to be retained to operate or secure the service.

Contact

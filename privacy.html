Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

This notice describes the information handled by Hospital Roster Builder and the choices available when using its staff scheduling workspace.

Information the workspace handles

When an authorized user signs in, the authentication service processes the account email and session information. The workspace may store staff names, optional UNID or employee IDs, roster titles, month and year, staff subtitles, manual row details, daily duty codes, leave dates and optional leave notes. Do not enter patient names, patient records or sensitive clinical details: this tool is for staff scheduling, not patient care records.

Where information is stored

Signed-in staff and roster records, assignments, saved rosters and leave entries are stored using the cloud database and sign-in services configured by the workspace operator. The current workspace is shared: provisioned signed-in accounts can access the same staff and roster data. It does not currently provide separate staff-only views or per-person roster permissions.

Some settings are saved in the browser, including custom roster titles and custom duty codes. Blank-row and subtitle values may also be cached locally while they are saved or synchronized with a roster. Browser storage stays on that device and may be removed by clearing its site data. Avoid using a shared device without signing out.

How information is used

Information is used to sign users in, maintain the shared staff directory, create and reopen monthly rosters, record leave, apply LE to affected roster dates, combine saved duties into a selected-date message, and produce print, PDF or Excel outputs. The application has no advertising or analytics feature configured.

Daily messages and exports

A daily message is assembled from saved roster data for the selected date, then shown in an editable preview. Copying it places the text on your device clipboard. Choosing Open in WhatsApp opens WhatsApp with a prepared message; the application does not send it automatically. Printing, downloading or sharing roster files is initiated by the user. Review the contents and recipients because exports may contain staff information.

Service providers

The application is delivered by its configured web host and uses configured providers for sign-in and cloud data. It loads interface fonts and software libraries from Google Fonts and jsDelivr. Those providers may receive technical connection information when your browser requests their services; their own privacy notices and terms apply to their services.

Access, correction and deletion

Use the workspace controls to correct staff details, remove a saved roster or delete a leave record. Contact the workspace administrator for help with account access or a data request. Removing a staff member from the active directory does not erase the historical staff snapshot stored in an already saved roster. Browser-only settings can be removed from the browser’s site data. Cloud information may remain until it is deleted through the workspace or handled by its administrator.

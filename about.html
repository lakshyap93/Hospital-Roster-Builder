WHAT IT INCLUDES

Planning tools for the whole month

Keep the roster editable while making the finished plan easy to save, review and share.

01

Staff directory

Maintain staff names and optional UNID or employee IDs. Edit or remove records as the team changes.

02

Flexible monthly rosters

Create separate rosters for different units or teams—even for the same month—then reopen them in roster history.

03

Fast duty entry

Use your duty codes, combine two shifts such as M/E, navigate with the keyboard, and reorder or copy staff rows.

04

Staff leave

Record a day or date range with an optional note. Matching roster dates are marked LE automatically.

05

Daily WhatsApp draft

Choose a date to merge duties from all saved rosters for that day. Review and edit the message before copying or opening WhatsApp.

06

Print and export

Prepare a formatted roster for printing, download PDF or Excel, and keep leave history as a printable or downloadable PDF.

01
STAFF DIRECTORY

Keep the team list current

Add each staff member’s name and, if needed, a UNID or employee ID. Use Edit to correct a name or ID, and Delete to remove someone from the active directory. Older saved rosters keep their staff snapshot for history.

Open Staff directory →
02
MONTHLY PLANNING

Create one roster for each team or unit

Choose or add a roster title, month and year. Add staff from the searchable directory; after selecting someone, focus returns to search for the next person. You can save separate rosters for the same month—for example, different wards or units.

Enter duty codes directly into each date cell. Built-in codes include M (Morning), E (Evening), N (Night), O (Off) and LE (Leave). You can use a combined code such as M/E when both component codes exist. Add a short staff subtitle, such as a unit or role, under a name; it appears in print/PDF and downloads.

Open Create roster →
03
KEYBOARD AND ROWS

Enter duties without leaving the grid

  • Use Tab to move through fields. In the duty grid, the arrow keys move left/right between dates and up/down between staff rows.
  • Focus a duty cell and press Delete to clear just that shift. Focus the staff name cell and press Delete to clear all duties in that row.
  • Drag a staff row to change its order. Hold Ctrl while dragging to copy the row to another staff member.
  • Click a staff name and press Ctrl+C to copy that row’s duties; click the destination staff name and press Ctrl+V to paste.
  • Blank rows can hold a typed name, optional ID and duties. They are saved with the roster when the required database table is set up.
04
STAFF LEAVE

Record a date or a date range once

Select a staff member, choose Leave from and Leave through, and add an optional note. Saved leave automatically marks that staff member as LE on matching dates in their roster and in the daily message. Check leave dates before saving or sharing a final schedule.

Open Staff leave →
05
DAILY DUTY MESSAGE

Merge every roster for a selected day

Choose any date, then generate the message. The page combines the saved roster duties for that date across all rosters for the month. Edit the preview, copy it, or open WhatsApp with the prepared draft. WhatsApp does not send until you review and send it there.

Open Daily duty message →
06
PRINT AND EXPORT

Use the format that suits the task

From a roster, prepare the print layout or download PDF and Excel. Roster name, staff ID and subtitle are included. The Staff leave page has its own print and PDF actions for leave history. Roster history lets you reopen, review or delete saved schedules.

Open Roster history →
ACCESS AND PRIVACY

Use the shared workspace carefully

This version has one signed-in shared workspace, not individual staff-only accounts. Signed-in users can access shared roster data. Use staff information only when authorized, keep notes minimal, and do not enter patient details. Sessions end automatically after six hours.
